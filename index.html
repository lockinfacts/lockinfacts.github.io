Real facts. Under a minute. A quiz in every video.

LockInFacts is an educational short-video channel. Each video tells one true story from history, AI and technology, or science, asks you a multiple-choice question, gives you three seconds to lock in your answer, and then reveals it.

How we make videos

✅ Every fact is checked against at least two reliable sources (encyclopaedias, museums, universities, established publishers), and the sources are listed under every video.

🖼️ Images come only from the public domain or carry an open licence, with credit given.

🤖 Our host is an original 3D robot. The narration is a synthetic (AI) voice, and we say so in every description.

🧭 We avoid political, religious and other controversial topics, and we correct any mistake we find.

About the LockInFacts Uploader

The LockInFacts Uploader is a private tool used only by the LockInFacts team to publish our own finished videos, with their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, to our own YouTube channel (through YouTube API Services) and our own Instagram account. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account. See our Privacy Policy.
