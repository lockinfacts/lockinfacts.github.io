Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Who we are

LockInFacts is an educational video channel on YouTube and Instagram. This policy covers this website and the LockInFacts Uploader, our internal tool for publishing our own videos. Contact: lockinfacts@gmail.com.

2. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and it has no forms. We do not collect personal information from visitors. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. The LockInFacts Uploader and YouTube API Services

The LockInFacts Uploader uses YouTube API Services. It is used only by the LockInFacts team, signed in to our own Google account, to upload our own finished videos to our own YouTube channel. It does not let anyone else sign in and does not access any other user's account or data.

  • What it accesses: permission to upload videos and set thumbnails on our channel (youtube.upload), and read-only access to our own channel's name and handle (youtube.readonly) so the tool can confirm it is posting to the correct channel.
  • What it stores: the sign-in token Google issues to us, and the IDs and links of the videos we upload. These are stored only on our own computer, in a private folder, and are never sent anywhere except to Google to make the upload.
  • What it does not do: it does not collect, store, sell or share any viewer, subscriber or third-party data, and it does not read comments, analytics or anyone's personal information.
  • Deleting data: we can delete the stored token at any time, and revoke the tool's access to the Google account at Google security settings. Anyone can manage apps connected to their own Google account on that page.

By using YouTube, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Instagram

The same tool publishes our own videos to our own Instagram account through Meta's Instagram API. It accesses only our account, and stores only our own access token and the IDs of our posts. Meta's handling of data is described in the Instagram Data Policy.

5. Viewers on YouTube and Instagram

When you watch, like or comment on our videos, that happens on YouTube or Instagram and is covered by their own privacy policies. We only see what those platforms show every channel owner (for example, public comments and total view counts).

6. Children

Our content is made for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
